On a proprietary binary forecasting task (about a thousand held-out cases), the unpenalised residual variant degraded its prior's held-out log-loss by 0.0060 nats (paired block-bootstrap SE 0.0030). The output-space penalty removed that degradation (penalised model minus prior: -0.0001 nats, SE 0.0007), halved the seed-to-seed spread of predicted probabilities (0.008 to 0.004) and lowered ECE from 0.023 to 0.014. The residual did not beat the prior by a significant margin on that task.
